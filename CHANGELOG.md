# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 9 August 2026

Streamlined prototype release focused on the reviewer and policy-planning journey.

- Reduced primary navigation from seven items to five: Overview, Build scenario, Results, Compare and Assumptions.
- Simplified the dashboard to four core decision outputs.
- Reorganised the scenario builder into three short steps with advanced assumptions collapsed by default.
- Consolidated results into Overview, Uptake & reach, Cost & capacity, Equity and Advanced.
- Moved the optimiser, detailed reports, methods and technical controls out of the primary navigation.
- Simplified evidence provenance and scenario comparison views.
- Improved mobile layout, visual hierarchy, touch targets, focus treatment and progressive disclosure.
- Retained the full analytical engines, saved scenarios, exports, PWA support and reproducibility features.

## 0.9.0 - 8 August 2026

Initial full-feature research prototype.
