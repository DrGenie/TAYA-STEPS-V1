# Evidence notes

TAYA-STEPS uses four evidence grades: A official Australian source, B direct peer-reviewed evidence, C literature-informed translation, and D prototype assumption.

The 2026 context register includes the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare youth mental-health page, eSafety social media age restriction material and evaluation, Healthdirect BRAVE information, Medicare Benefits Schedule references and Australian Government Health Design System guidance.

Peer-reviewed context includes Wittevrongel and colleagues on youth mental-health service preferences and barriers, Henderson and colleagues on integrated youth services, DCE methodological guidance, adolescent anxiety treatment evidence and budget-impact good practice.

Published preference evidence is used to inform directions and plausible relative importance. Numerical TAYA-STEPS coefficients remain calibrated synthetic coefficients because source studies differ in attributes, populations, currencies and utility scales.

All editable core assumptions have in-app evidence records with rationale and source links. Grade C and D inputs must not be described as observed data.
